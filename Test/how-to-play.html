How to Play

Welcome to Journey to Welch! This short fantasy adventure game will take you through a quest based in a murky dungeon. The game is set in the land Kingdom of Connecticut, where the player is set on a journey to deliver a message to King Welch. This playthrough is simply the beginning of a massive idea the development team had. Enjoy this dungeon crawl!

Objective

Your goal for this playthrough is to escape the Murky Dungeon by fighting your way through!

Controls

  • Throughout the game, you will be prompted to enter a word like "start" or "next" to continue the game, allowing the player to play at their own pace. Just enter the text, and hit enter, or the on-screen submit button!
  • When in combat, input the numbers 1 (Attack), 2 (Defend), or 3 (Magic) to choose your fight choice, and hit enter or the on-screen submit button.
  • Choose your fight choice carefully, they all have different advantages/disadvantages!
  • Once you start playing, there is a more in-depth combat tutorial to get you ready for your Journey to Welch!
